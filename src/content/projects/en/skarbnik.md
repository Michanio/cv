Web app for school class treasurers and parent councils: contributions, trip collections and shared costs settled per school year, with balances per child, one-click payment reminders, and role-based access for treasurers, secretaries, observers and parents (parents see only their own child). It is also my testbed for AI-assisted engineering, exploring how AI tools change the way software is designed and built.
